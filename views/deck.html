The problem

Growth is a black box

Many companies want to grow more but do not know exactly how. Often they do not know how they have grown so far either. Growth is rarely understood or systematic. When it looks like this, it is hard to repeat, hard to scale and hard to fix when it stops.

03

What is growth engineering

Growth can be a system you can

Growth engineering is the discipline of designing and running growth as a system, instead of a series of disconnected attempts. It is how the most competitive companies grow today. Our team brings experience from some of the most advanced growth teams, and works alongside yours to get there.

measure

Every step, from first contact to sale. You know where results come from.

optimise

Weak points show up in the numbers and get fixed, week after week.

count on

An engine that runs every day. Growth comes from the system, not from luck.

scale

It returns more than it costs, and the numbers show it. The more you put in, the more you get out.

(01)DAB PumpsManufacturing · Water technologies

Demand pulled through the whole chain

A world leader in water technologies. Its products reach the end client through long chains of distributors, installers and specifiers, and each of them shapes the choice. With DAB's teams, we designed, built and run integrated growth engines across product lines and markets: one system that reads signals along the chain, activates the right stakeholder at the right moment and connects brand, demand and sales on the same data. Demand is pulled through the chain instead of pushed, and every activity is measured on what it sells.

The company
A world leader in water technologies.
The goal
Grow faster through long distribution chains.
The system we built
Integrated growth engines across product lines and markets: demand generation, DTC and ABM, from brand to sales.
  • Signal-based activation
  • Account-based marketing
  • Installer and specifier programmes
  • Demand generation
  • D2C
  • Creative production
  • Marketing automation
  • Sales alignment
  • Revenue attribution

(02)vVardisHealthcare · Dental

Dental practices and patients, moving together

A Swiss unicorn with a drill-free treatment for early caries: a new category in oral care. To make it the new standard, dental practices and patients have to move together. We designed, built and run one system that connects both sides: it finds the practices most ready to adopt, guides them from first discovery to the partnership pilot, and brings patients who want the treatment into those same practices. One data layer, from the first signal to the first treatment, measured on adoption and revenue.

The company
A Swiss unicorn with a drill-free treatment for caries.
The goal
Make it the new standard in dental practices.
The system we built
The path that takes dental practices from discovering the treatment to joining the pilot, and brings patients to those practices.
  • Practice targeting
  • Adoption journey
  • Sales handover
  • Patient demand
  • Local activation
  • B2B and B2C on one data layer
  • AI automation
  • Full-funnel attribution

Every channel, one customer journey
